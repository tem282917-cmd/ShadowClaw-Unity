Adaptations for Shadow: original aligned instrumental stems were remixed into
exploration/combat and normal/blackout arrangements. Levels were adjusted and
audio was converted from 48 kHz 24-bit to 48 kHz 16-bit stereo WAV for Unity import.
Original timing, pitches, and author-provided start/end loop points are retained.
Each loop edge has a 3 ms continuity correction to remove a sample discontinuity.
Music by Leonardo Calvo; no endorsement by the composer is implied.
These music assets remain available under CC BY 4.0.

Title Veil adapts the Piano and Glocken stems from Cursed by Leonardo Calvo.
The title arrangement rolls off bass and treble, softens the note attacks,
and leaves space around the piano with a quiet stereo echo.
