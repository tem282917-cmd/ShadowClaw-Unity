# Fire FX – Burning Fire Animation

Mikodrak published the looping fire animation from Nyrthos under the CC0 1.0 Universal public domain dedication. [Original work and license](https://opengameart.org/content/fire-fx-burning-fire-animation) · [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)

The gallery lanterns and the fire along the demon's shadow use the front layer from [the author's FIRE.zip](https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/FIRE.zip). ShadowClaw packs the 40 numbered RGBA frames into an eight-column, five-row sheet. Each source frame retains its original 81 × 123 pixels inside a padded 88 × 128 cell.
